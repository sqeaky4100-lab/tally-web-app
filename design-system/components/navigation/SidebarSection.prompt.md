An area heading in the rail, grouping the projects beneath it.

```jsx
<SidebarSection label="Work" icon="briefcase">
  <SidebarItem progress={0.4} label="Prepare Presentation" indent={1} />
</SidebarSection>
```

Areas are never counted or badged — only the projects inside them are. Give each section 20px of air above it; that space is the entire visual separation between areas.
