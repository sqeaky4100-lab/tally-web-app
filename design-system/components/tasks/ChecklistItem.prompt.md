A sub-step inside a to-do's detail card — a shopping list, the stages of a process.

```jsx
<ChecklistItem label="Introduction needs more images" />
<ChecklistItem label="Add our company logo" checked />
<ChecklistItem label="Animate the build" last />
```

Uses a small blue-outlined circle rather than the square to-do box, so a checklist never reads as a nested list of to-dos. Hairline rules separate items; a drag handle fades in on hover.
